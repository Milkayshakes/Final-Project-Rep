# HMA (Human Made Art)

## Repository
<https://github.com/Milkayshakes/Final-Project-Rep>

## Description
If you have been on the internet, you know that the world is currently ran by AI slop using stories to make the generrated content seem interesting. I want to create a software for real artists and storytellers without the use of AI content. By the end of this I will have a creative format for a storyteller to write their story and a collabrative artist to come in and finish their story with art. 

## Features
- Feature 1
	- This will be a social media platform similar to Instagram and other collaborative aspects of social media
- Feature 2
	- There will be two roles, there is the writer and the artist (Can of course switch and swap) One of the roles will create a beginning post that is human made. The post will be put on the social platform waiting for collaborative work.
- Feature N 
	- There will be a home page that has all of these collaborative options. Once someone accepts one of these posts, it will be placed on their own collaborative page, the artist or writer will finish the opposite titles work and submit it to create a finished outcome. There will also be a way to chat with others through DM's. 

## Challenges
- Must figure out a way to be able to have multiple people be able to work on the same page.
- Be able to figure out a way to have the account handle a massive amout of read/write operations without crashing or acting slow
- Figure out the hybrid data model when it comes to social media platforms, as well as figure out how to create a graph database for recommendations

## Outcomes
Ideal Outcome:
- The best case scenario would be to have a entire working social media platform where you can easily collaborate with other creators and have the ability to meet and work with other creators without the use of AI.

Minimal Viable Outcome:
- The bare minimum would be to have a smaller example of this program to show that my idea works rather than an entire social media application.
